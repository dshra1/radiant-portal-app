03Plumbing

  • Supply on both floors is the floors 4–5 bore zone with the tank raised 10 ft: 1¼″ down-take per duct, 1″ branch with a ball valve into each wet room, ¾″ to each shower mixer and ½″ to other points. Expect about 0.4–0.5 bar on the 5th floor, so specify low-pressure shower fittings there.
  • Municipal water goes to the two RO points only, through the ½″ down-take in S2. Bore and municipal pipes never connect.
  • Wet rooms need about 100 mm build-up above the slab for side-outlet drains. Waterproof the slab and the top of the fill, and pond-test both.
  • The 5th-floor toilet and both powder rooms have no window: their exhaust fans duct into the N1 / N2 shafts, so the north face stays exactly as on the typical floors.
  • This relies on N2 being kept in the 3BHK set, as A100-3 shows. If N2 is dropped, the 5th-floor toilet needs a ledge of about 8 ft back to N1 instead.

04Electrical points

06For the elders on the 5th floor

  • Everyday entry is the 5th-floor door from the lift, so no stairs are needed day to day.
  • Bell pushes at the bedside and a pull-cord in the master toilet, sounding in the long hall and kitchen.
  • Low-level night lights between the bed and the toilet door; two-way switching at the bedside.
  • Grab bars at the WC and in the shower, fold-down shower seat, R11 anti-skid tiles, a flush shower entry with the linear drain, lever taps and lever door handles.
  • Toilet doors open outward, as on the typical floor, so a fall behind the door never blocks it.

07Points to confirm

  • N2 stays in the final 3BHK set (needed for the moved toilet and powder).
  • Beams over the removed walls (hall, living, lounge, drawing) show as ceiling drops at about 8′10″; the false-ceiling design should run with them.
  • North-face outdoor-unit brackets at the office need a screen and the architect's agreement on the road elevation.
  • Vastu consultant to sign off the moved toilet (north-centre) and the furniture orientations.
  • TSSPDCL: keep both flat connections at 18 kW each, or merge into one supply if preferred.
